Student research route

Turn a good question into usable evidence.

This guided brief helps students propose a local focus, comparison, source or perspective without blurring research leads and verified facts.

The page stores and sends nothing itself. When you finish, it opens your email app with a structured draft for you to review.

Step 1 of 4

Who and where

A display name is optional. Contact details are used only for editorial follow-up and are not published automatically.

Use one locality, not a whole country.

Step 2 of 4

The research idea

Say whether this is a fact claim, interpretation, reflection or research lead.

Step 3 of 4

Sources and boundaries

A full profile needs at least two reliable sources. One source is enough for an initial lead, but it will remain under review.

Step 4 of 4

Permission and editorial review

You can review and change everything before sending.

A publishable profile needs

  • One named locality abroad
  • Three to five sourced comparisons
  • At least two reliable sources
  • A student perspective
  • Fact separated from reflection
  • Permissions recorded

Editorial route

  1. Submission is received as a research lead.
  2. An editor checks sources and claim boundaries.
  3. People and media permissions are confirmed.
  4. Status and review details appear on the page.

Privacy by design

This static page does not collect, save or transmit form data. It prepares a draft in your own email app.
