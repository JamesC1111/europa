A pairing becomes a shared investigation

The government country pairing is the starting point. Students choose a specific overseas locality and build the page together.

  1. 01

    Choose locally

    Pair the Irish county with a named region, city, department or county-equivalent—not a whole country.

  2. 02

    Research together

    Student teams find three to five sourced comparisons, meaningful differences and one consented perspective.

  3. 03

    Publish responsibly

    Claims carry sources and a visible status. Fact, reflection and open questions never blur together.

Transparency across contributions

Every profile shows what is known—and what is not.

  • Verified Source is fact-checked.
  • Under review Statement is unverified or anecdotal.
  • Research lead A promising question, not an established fact.
