A place to begin

From the Atlantic edge to the heart of Europe.

Cork is officially paired with France in Ireland's 2026 EU County Pairings initiative. This page is a starting point for students to notice connections, ask better questions, and build a richer public record.

About the history: Huguenot links are a possible historical connection worth researching and sourcing further. They are not presented here as the official reason for the pairing.

How the atlas grows

A pairing becomes a page through student collaboration.

This is not a list of destinations. Each page is a shared study of two places: what they hold in common, where they differ, and the stories that connect them.

  1. 01

    Match the places

    Start with an Irish county and a named local area abroad.

  2. 02

    Research together

    Student contributors bring knowledge, questions, comparisons, and reliable sources.

  3. 03

    Share a living record

    Publish a careful page that can deepen over time as new voices and evidence arrive.
